How the model was tested

The size curve is the only fitted term with a ground truth — live tokens' market caps — so it is the term that gets held-out tests. Every test refits the ensemble on one part of the live tokens and scores it on protocols it never saw. The miss is the median factor between the predicted and the true market cap. The flat 0.25 multiple is the control.
